## The Honest Fee Landscape in Sonipat

I get asked "why are you the most expensive" almost every week, usually by a parent sitting across the table with a calculator in their head. Fair question. Here is the market as it actually looks, not as anyone's brochure describes it.

**Budget computer centres (₹15,000 to ₹20,000).** These are the neighbourhood computer institutes that added "digital marketing" to their signboard alongside tally, typing, and basic computer courses. The teaching is usually generic slides, no live campaigns, and the person taking your class may be teaching five unrelated subjects in the same week.

**Mid-range local institutes (₹25,000 to ₹35,000).** A step up. Some live teaching, a proper syllabus, occasionally a trainer with real industry background. What is usually missing is scale: small or no real ad budgets to practice on, and placement support that is more of a promise than a process.

**Digital Magician (₹45,000, reduced from ₹60,000).** Our full 4-month program, Monday to Friday, 90 minutes a day across four available slots. I teach every batch personally. Students work inside live client ad accounts where our team manages ₹1 crore-plus in real annual ad spend, not dummy dashboards. You leave with 10+ Google and Meta certifications and a placement guarantee backed by a written refund clause.

**National online platforms (₹50,000 and above).** Big brand names, polished video content, and a genuinely capable curriculum on paper. What you usually do not get is anyone who knows your name, any local placement push, or hands-on time with a real account and real money on the line.

Here is that comparison as a table, because this is the part worth saving.

| Fee band | What you typically get | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 (budget computer centres) | Generic slides, no live accounts, a generalist teacher, a certificate at the end | Someone who wants basic awareness only, not a job |
| ₹25,000 to ₹35,000 (mid-range local institutes) | Some live teaching, limited or shared ad budgets, informal placement help | Someone testing the waters with a bit more structure than free YouTube content |
| ₹45,000 (Digital Magician, Sonipat) | Founder-led teaching, live client accounts with real budgets, small batches, written placement guarantee | Someone who wants to be job-ready in 4 months and has a Sonipat presence to lean on |
| ₹50,000+ (national online platforms) | Strong recorded content, brand recognition, self-paced structure | Someone comfortable with remote-only learning and no local placement support |

## What Actually Differs Between the Tiers

Fee comparisons are useless if you do not know what the money is actually buying at each level. Four things separate these tiers, and none of them show up on a fee receipt.

**Who is actually teaching you.** At the budget end, you often get whoever is free that hour, teaching a subject they read up on last week. In our [digital marketing course in Sonipat](/digital-marketing-course/sonipat), I teach every single batch myself. Not a rotating cast of trainers, not a recorded lecture with a moderator reading chat messages. Me, live, every day.

**Whether you touch a live ad account with real money.** This is the single biggest gap between cheap and serious training. Reading about Google Ads bidding strategies teaches you vocabulary. Managing a live campaign where a wrong setting burns a client's actual rupees teaches you judgment. Our trainers collectively manage more than ₹1 crore a year in ad spend across real client accounts, and students work inside that, not a sandbox account with fake data.

**Batch size.** A room of 60 students watching a screen is not training, it is a lecture. Smaller batches mean I can actually see who is stuck on which concept and fix it before it compounds into a bad habit that shows up in an interview three months later.

**Whether placement support is a commitment or a courtesy.** Almost every institute says "placement assistance." Very few put a number and a consequence on it. Ours is a written guarantee with a refund clause if we do not deliver. That document exists because I would rather lose money on a rare miss than let "placement support" mean a WhatsApp group full of job postings you could have found yourself.

We have placed 500+ students, and our Google reviews sit at 4.9 across 122 reviews at the time of writing. Neither number means anything if you cannot verify the process behind it, which is why the guarantee is written, not verbal.

## When You Should NOT Pay ₹45,000

I would rather lose your enrolment now than have you regret it in month two. There are real situations where our course is not the right buy.

**You only want the basics for your own shop.** If you run a small local business and just want to post better on Instagram and understand what your ad budget is doing, you do not need a 4-month job-readiness program. A budget-tier short course, or even structured self-learning, covers that ground fine.

**You are only testing interest.** If you are not sure digital marketing is even the field you want, do not commit ₹45,000 to find out. Sit through a lower-cost or free introduction first. Come back to a serious program once you know this is the path you actually want to build a career on.

**You cannot commit the daily 90 minutes.** Our schedule runs Monday to Friday, 90 minutes a day, across four slots so you can pick what fits. But if your work, college, or family situation genuinely cannot free up that time consistently, you will not get the outcome the fee is built around, and that is on the schedule, not the content.
