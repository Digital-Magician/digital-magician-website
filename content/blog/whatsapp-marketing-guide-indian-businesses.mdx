If you've run both an email list and a WhatsApp broadcast to the same Sonipat customer base, you already know the gap: email open rates for small local businesses hover in single digits, WhatsApp read rates run north of 90%. For a business that lives on repeat customers and local trust, that difference isn't marginal. It's the whole game.

## Why WhatsApp Beats Email for Local Indian Businesses

India has over [500 million WhatsApp users](https://www.statista.com/statistics/280914/number-of-whatsapp-users-in-india/), and for most of them it's the first app they open, not the last. A coaching centre in Sonipat sending a fee-reminder email might get read three days later, if at all. The same reminder on WhatsApp gets read within the hour, often within minutes.

That's not because WhatsApp is a better platform in some abstract sense. It's because Indian customers, especially outside metros, treat WhatsApp as their primary communication channel the way an American consumer might treat SMS or email. A shop, a clinic, a coaching centre, a real estate agent: all of them get faster replies and higher recall on WhatsApp than on any other channel. ## WhatsApp Business App vs WhatsApp Business API

The first decision every business owner faces is which version of WhatsApp to build on. They're not the same product, and picking the wrong one wastes both time and money.

| Feature | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business API |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free | Free platform access, but Meta charges per conversation |
| Who it suits | Solo owners, small shops, under 1,000 contacts | Growing businesses, multiple team members, automation needs |
| Sending | Manual, one device at a time | Automated, connects to CRM and chatbots |
| Broadcast limit | 256 contacts per list, must have saved your number | No hard limit, works with opt-in lists of any size |
| Team access | Single device (or linked devices, limited) | Multiple agents, shared inbox tools |
| Catalog | Yes, built in | Yes, plus product sync with e-commerce platforms |
| Setup | Download and go | Needs a Business Solution Provider or Meta approval |

If you run a single shop or coaching centre and message under a thousand people a month, the free app is genuinely enough. Don't pay for the API until manual sending starts costing you more time than the automation would save.

## The Legit Playbook: What Actually Works

**Catalogs.** Set up your product or service catalog inside WhatsApp Business. A customer browsing your catalog and messaging you directly from a product convert far better than one bouncing off a generic homepage.

**Quick replies and greeting messages.** Save your five most-asked questions as quick replies. Fee structure, timings, location, availability, price range. This alone cuts response time from hours to seconds.

**Broadcast lists to opted-in customers only.** This is where most businesses get it wrong, and where compliance and effectiveness point the same direction. Only message people who've saved your number or explicitly opted in. A broadcast to 200 genuinely interested customers outperforms a blast to 5,000 strangers every time, because the read rate and reply rate on an opted-in list are dramatically higher.

**Click-to-WhatsApp ads from Meta.** Run a Facebook or Instagram ad that opens directly into a WhatsApp conversation instead of a landing page. For local businesses, this consistently outperforms traditional lead-form ads because the customer starts talking to a real person immediately. We build these campaigns as part of our [Meta Ads course in Sonipat](/meta-ads-course/sonipat), because the ad and the WhatsApp follow-up are really one connected funnel, not two separate steps.

**Status marketing.** WhatsApp Status functions like Instagram Stories, and for a local business with an engaged customer base, it's an underused free channel. Post offers, new stock, or behind-the-scenes updates a few times a week.

## Message Templates That Work for Indian SMBs

Keep messages short, specific, and easy to act on. Three that we've refined across client accounts:

**Appointment reminder:**
"Hi [Name], this is a reminder for your appointment tomorrow at [time] with [Business]. Reply YES to confirm or call us to reschedule."

**Offer message:**
"[Name], we're running a [X]% discount on [product/service] this week only, valid till [date]. Reply here or visit us to claim it."

**Follow-up after a visit or purchase:**
"Hi [Name], thanks for visiting [Business] last week. How's [product/service] working out for you? Let us know if you need anything, we're just a message away."

Each of these has a clear purpose, a name for personalization, and a simple next action. That combination is what separates a message customers read from one they mute.

## What Gets You Banned

Meta bans WhatsApp Business numbers aggressively, and the reasons are almost always the same three things.

**Buying number lists.** Every list sold as "10,000 verified WhatsApp numbers" is a fast route to a ban, because those numbers never opted in to hear from you, and enough of them will report you to trigger Meta's spam detection.

**Cold bulk blasting.** Sending the same promotional message to thousands of unknown numbers gets flagged within days, sometimes hours. WhatsApp tracks block rates and report rates closely, and a spike shuts the number down.

**Third-party bulk senders outside Meta's approved system.** Tools that spoof the WhatsApp Business app to send mass messages violate WhatsApp's terms directly. We've seen business owners lose a number with years of customer history overnight because they used one of these tools to save a few thousand rupees.

The businesses that get banned are almost never the ones running small, opted-in broadcasts. They're the ones chasing scale through shortcuts.

## Realistic Costs

The WhatsApp Business app costs ₹0. Download it, verify your number, and start messaging customers who've saved you. No subscription, no per-message fee.

The WhatsApp Business API works differently: Meta charges per conversation (a 24-hour window), with different rates for marketing, utility, and authentication conversations. On top of that, most businesses pay a Business Solution Provider a monthly fee for the dashboard, automation, and support. For a small or mid-size Sonipat business, budget somewhere in the low thousands of rupees a month once you're on the API, scaling up with volume.

## Measuring What Actually Matters

Track three numbers, not vanity metrics. Read rate (are people opening your messages), reply rate (are they engaging), and conversion rate (are they actually booking, buying, or visiting). A broadcast with a 95% read rate and a 2% conversion rate tells you the message is fine but the offer isn't. A broadcast with a low read rate tells you your list has gone stale, or you're messaging people who never really opted in.
