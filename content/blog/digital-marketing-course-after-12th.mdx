Every June, after the board results, I get the same message dozens of times: *"Sir, I just finished 12th. Is digital marketing a good career? Should I do a course now or finish my degree first?"*

I've placed 500+ students in digital marketing jobs, and a large chunk of them started right after Class 12. So let me give you the honest version — not the brochure version.

## Can You Really Start Digital Marketing After 12th?

Yes. And it's one of the few high-paying careers where your Class 12 stream genuinely does not matter.

I've placed students from science, commerce, and arts backgrounds into the same roles, at the same salaries. Digital marketing is a **skill-based field, not a degree-based one.** A hiring manager in Gurgaon does not ask whether you took PCM or Commerce. They ask one thing: *can you run a campaign and show me the results?*

That's good news if your board marks weren't great. The entire industry is built on what you can *do*, not what you scored.

## Course After 12th vs Finishing Your Degree First

This is the real question, so let me be direct about the three honest paths:

**1. Do a degree AND a digital marketing course in parallel.** This is what I recommend for most students. Enrol in a regular BA/BBA/B.Com (even distance/correspondence) for the degree, and do a serious digital marketing course alongside. By the time you graduate at 21, you already have 2–3 years of real skills and a portfolio. You walk into the job market years ahead of your batchmates.

**2. Course first, earn while you study.** Plenty of my students started freelancing or working part-time within months of starting the course, then funded their own degree with that income. If money is tight at home, this path turns the equation around.

**3. Degree first, course later.** The "safe" traditional route. It's fine — but understand you're delaying your skill-building by three years, and a degree alone in 2026 gets very few interviews.

> The students who win are almost never the ones who waited. They're the ones who started building real skills at 18 while everyone else was "just doing graduation."

## The One Mistake to Avoid

Don't pick a course because it's cheap and hands you a certificate. After 12th, you have time on your side — use it to learn from people who *actually run campaigns*, on *real budgets*, not trainers reading slides.

When you compare courses, ask one question: **"Will I work on real client campaigns, or just dummy accounts?"** That single difference decides whether you can answer interview questions a year from now.

## Your Path From Class 12 to a Paying Job

If I had to map the fastest realistic route, it's this:

1. Enrol in a skills-first digital marketing course (alongside a degree if you can)
2. Build a portfolio of real campaigns *during* the course, not after
3. Earn your Google and Meta certifications
4. Apply to 30+ roles with proof of results — or start freelancing locally
5. Land your first job, usually within 30–60 days of finishing

That's the path. It works because it's built on skills employers can verify, not certificates they ignore.
