The live online batch is the same class we teach in Model Town, Sonipat, attended through a screen. Same trainer, same live client accounts, same four daily slots, same fee of ₹45,000 or ₹40,000 paid in full. It is not a recorded course with a support group attached, and the difference matters more than most people expect.

I resisted running it for two years because I thought online teaching would be worse. What changed my mind was watching a student from Gohana spend three hours a day on buses and fall behind, not because the material was hard, but because she was exhausted.

![A student at home in the evening joining a live online class on a laptop, notebook and chai beside her](/blog-images/live-online-digital-marketing-batch-india.jpg)

## What a day actually looks like

Classes run Monday to Friday, ninety minutes, in four slots: 7:30 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:30 PM and 5:30 PM. You pick the one that fits your work or college and stay in it.

A typical session is not a lecture. The first ten minutes clear up questions from yesterday's practice. Then roughly forty minutes of live work, which usually means my screen is shared and we are inside a real account, changing something and watching what happens. After that, you do the same thing on your own account while we are still on the call, so when it breaks, someone is there.

You leave with a task. Not a quiz, a task: build the campaign structure, write five ad variations, pull the search terms report and tell me which three keywords are wasting money. The next class begins with those answers.

## What you get access to

- **The live class**, in your chosen slot, five days a week for four months.
- **Recordings of every session**, kept for 12 months, which exist for revision and missed days rather than as the main course.
- **Live client ad accounts**, supervised, so you practise with real budgets instead of dummy data.
- **A private student group** where questions get answered between sessions, usually by other students first.
- **Portfolio reviews**, where your work gets picked apart before an employer does it.
- **The two month internship** after training, which is where most of the confidence actually arrives.
- **Placement support**: interview preparation, the job portal, and placement drives, all run online as well.

## Online or classroom, honestly compared

| | Live online | Model Town classroom |
|---|---|---|
| Trainer and syllabus | Identical | Identical |
| Fee | ₹45,000, or ₹40,000 paid in full | Same |
| Live client accounts | Yes | Yes |
| Travel | None | 15 to 40 minutes for most of Sonipat |
| Asking a half-formed question | Harder | Easier |
| Learning from others' mistakes | You hear them | You see them |
| Discipline required | Considerably more | Less |
| Placement support | Same terms | Same terms |

The two rows that matter are the last few. What you lose online is the sideways conversation: the question you would ask the person next to you but will not type into a group, and the accident of overhearing someone else's problem, which is often how you learn the thing you did not know existed.

What you lose in the classroom is the travel, and for students outside Sonipat that is frequently the deciding factor. If you are in Gohana, Kharkhoda, Panipat or further, the honest recommendation is online. Keep the hours and spend them practising instead of commuting.

## Who this works for, and who struggles

It works well for working people. The 7:30 AM slot before a shift and the 5:30 PM slot after one are the two fullest batches we run, and those students tend to be the most motivated because they are paying with their own time.

It works for students in smaller towns across Haryana, Punjab, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh who want live training without relocating. It works for women whose families are not comfortable with daily travel, which is a real constraint in this region and not one I am going to pretend away.

It struggles for people who need the room to hold them to it. If you have started three online courses and finished none, the honest answer is that the classroom is worth the commute for you. Knowing which kind you are is worth more than any syllabus comparison. There is a fuller breakdown in the [online versus offline](/blog/online-vs-offline-digital-marketing-course-sonipat) post.

## The technical requirements, which are modest

A laptop, not just a phone. Ten megabits of stable connection. Headphones, because unmuting in a house with a television on is its own problem. That is all. Everything we teach runs in a browser: Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, Search Console, Analytics, the AI tools.

If your connection drops during a session, the recording is there the same evening. If it drops often, tell us and we will work out which slot has the least contention in your area.

## What the first month covers

Month one is foundations and SEO: how search works, keyword research, on-page work, and your first live audit of a real business. Month two is paid: Google Ads and Meta Ads, structure, creative, budgets, reading the numbers. Month three is automation, analytics and AI applied across both. Month four is client work and portfolio, which is also when the interview preparation starts.

## Placement, in writing

The guarantee applies to online students on identical terms. Complete every module, attend at least 75% of the live classes, finish your portfolio project, apply to 30 or more jobs through our portal within four months of finishing, and join two placement drives. If you have done all of that and you are still not placed, we refund the fee in full.

Around 80% of graduates are placed within 30 days. The full conditions are on the [placement guarantee](/placement) page, and I would read them before you read anyone's headline promise, including mine.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the online batch the same as the classroom one?**
It is the same batch, attended remotely. Same trainer, same accounts, same fee.

**What if I miss a class?**
Every session is recorded with 12 month access. The guarantee requires 75% attendance, so recordings are for catching up, not for replacing the class.

**What do I need technically?**
A laptop and a stable 10 Mbps connection. A phone alone will not work for ads manager tasks.

**Do online students get placement support?**
Yes, identical terms, and several now work remotely for NCR employers from smaller towns.

**Can I switch between online and classroom?**
Yes. Some students attend online during the week and come in for portfolio reviews. Tell us and we will arrange it.
