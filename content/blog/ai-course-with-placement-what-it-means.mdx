Most AI courses advertising placement are selling a hope, not a commitment. The difference is a document. Ask for the placement policy in writing, read the conditions, and you will know within two minutes which kind you are looking at.

I will give you ours in full further down so you have something to compare against, including the parts that are inconvenient for me.

## First, the uncomfortable part about AI jobs

Before the guarantee question, there is a bigger one that almost nobody selling AI courses will answer plainly.

At fresher level in Haryana and Delhi NCR, there is essentially no job titled AI executive. Search for one. What exists is machine learning engineer, which wants a computer science degree and real Python, and then a long list of marketing, content, design and analyst roles where AI skill is expected but is not the job title.

So an AI course that promises AI placement is promising a job market that does not exist at entry level. What a good one can honestly promise is that you become employable in a real role, with AI as the thing that makes you faster than the next candidate.

That is why AI at Digital Magician is taught inside the four month [digital marketing course in Sonipat](/digital-marketing-course/sonipat) rather than sold as a standalone certificate. The reasoning is set out at more length in [AI course in Sonipat](/blog/ai-course-in-sonipat).

## Guarantee, assistance, or a line on a poster

| What it is called | What it usually means | What to ask |
|---|---|---|
| Placement guarantee | Written commitment with conditions and a refund | Show me the policy and the conditions |
| Placement assistance | They share openings, no obligation | What happens if nothing comes of it? |
| 100% placement | Marketing line, rarely documented | What happened to last batch's students who were not placed? |
| Job guarantee | Varies wildly, read it carefully | Is the refund automatic or discretionary? |
| Internship guarantee | Often unpaid, sometimes internal | Is it paid, with a client, and how many hours? |

## The conditions that are fair, and the ones that are traps

Every real guarantee has conditions. That is not a trick. Nobody can place a student who stopped attending in week three and never applied anywhere.

**Reasonable:** attendance around 70% to 80%, completed assignments, a finished portfolio project, a stated number of applications within a stated window, attendance at placement drives.

**Traps:** 100% attendance, which nobody achieves. Undefined phrases like sincere effort. Any offer, any city, any salary, which lets them close the claim by offering you ₹9,000 in another state. Refunds at management discretion, which means no refund. Criteria that are not written down anywhere you can read before paying.

## What makes the placement actually work

A guarantee is a safety net, and nobody picks a course for the net. What matters is whether the process behind it works.

Three things decide it. Whether you have campaign work to show, because every interview turns on that. Whether the institute has employers who genuinely hire from them, as opposed to logos on a wall. And whether anyone prepares you for the interview, which is where most capable students lose offers.

When a student of ours does not get placed quickly, it is almost never the market. It is that they finished the course without a portfolio because they skipped the project, or they applied to four jobs and called it trying.

## The question to ask that nobody expects

Ask this: "How many students in your last completed batch did not get placed, and what happened to them?"

There is always someone. An institute that says nobody is either lying or has not run enough batches. The way they talk about the students who struggled tells you far more than the success stories, which are curated by definition.

There is a fuller checklist, including what to verify and how, in [how to check placement claims](/blog/check-placement-claims-sonipat-institute).

## Frequently asked questions

**Are AI placement guarantees genuine?**
Some are. Ask for the written policy with conditions. The reaction to that request tells you most of what you need.

**What job does an AI course lead to here?**
At fresher level, marketing, content, analyst or design roles where AI skill is expected. Not a role called AI executive.

**Will I pay extra for tools or ad budget?**
Ask before enrolling. With us, no. Elsewhere, often yes, and it changes the real price.

**What conditions are fair?**
Around 75% attendance, completed work, a portfolio project, a defined number of applications, and attending drives.

**How long until placement?**
About 80% of our graduates within 30 days. Anyone promising a date rather than a range is guessing.
