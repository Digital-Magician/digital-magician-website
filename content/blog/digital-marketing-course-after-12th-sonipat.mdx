## What a 12th-Pass Student in Sonipat Actually Faces

Walk into any local college counselling office in Sonipat after boards and you'll hear the same three tracks. A BA or BCom degree, because "at least you'll have something on paper." SSC or bank exam coaching, because a government job still carries weight with parents here. Or the Delhi commute plan, where the assumption is that anything worth doing in marketing, media, or tech happens two hours away by bus or train.

All three are valid. None of them are wrong. But they share one problem: they all delay the point where you start earning a real, marketable skill. A BA takes three years before it even claims to make you employable. Government coaching can take two to four attempts before it pays off, if it pays off. And the Delhi commute eats two to four hours of your day before you've even started working.

Digital marketing skips that wait. You can start building income-generating skills within your first month after 12th, without leaving Sonipat and without giving up on your degree.

## Can You Really Learn This Alongside a Local College Degree in Sonipat?

Yes, and this is the part most families get wrong. They think it's course instead of college. It's course alongside college, and that's exactly how I recommend most Sonipat students structure it.

Our batches run Monday to Friday, 90 minutes a day, in one of four slots: 7:30 AM, 9:00 AM, 12:30 PM, or 5:30 PM. If you're attending a local college like Vaish College or a government college in Sonipat, you pick a slot around your lecture timetable. Plenty of our students train in the early 7:30 AM slot before heading to class, or the 5:30 PM slot after their last lecture ends.

You finish the 4-month Full Stack Digital Marketing program while your degree is still running in year one. By your second year of college, you already have Google and Meta certifications, live campaign experience, and a portfolio, things your classmates won't touch until final year placements, if at all.

## What Can You Realistically Earn in Your First 12 Months?

Here's the number families actually want. Be skeptical of anyone who promises a fixed figure at 18 with zero experience, because it depends on effort. But here's a realistic month-by-month picture based on what I've seen from Sonipat students who started right after 12th.

Months 1 to 4, you're in training. No income yet, this is the skill-building phase: campaigns, certifications, portfolio work. Months 4 to 6, most students pick up their first paid work, usually freelance projects for small Sonipat businesses (a local shop's Instagram, a coaching centre's Google Ads, a boutique's WhatsApp catalogue), earning ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 a month. Months 6 to 9, that typically grows to ₹8,000 to ₹20,000 a month as you take on two or three clients or a part-time role. By month 12, consistent students are around ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 a month, some higher if they land a full-time offer.

That's alongside college, not instead of it. It's pocket money that eventually outgrows what most part-time jobs in Sonipat pay a fresher, and it's built on a skill you keep for life, not an hourly wage that stops the moment you stop showing up.

## What Sonipat Parents Actually Worry About

I've sat through enough of these conversations to know the questions before they're asked. Let me answer them straight.

**"Is this a real degree?"** No, and I won't pretend it is. It's a skill certification, backed by 10+ Google and Meta certifications your child earns during the course. That's precisely why I tell every family here to keep the college degree running. The course adds a skill on top, it doesn't replace the paper your family wants.

**"What if it doesn't work out?"** We back the outcome with a placement guarantee that includes a written refund clause. It's conditional: your child needs to complete the course, submit the assignments, build a real portfolio, and apply to 30+ jobs. If they do that work and still don't get placed, the refund clause kicks in. I'd rather families know the conditions upfront than discover them later.

**"Is 18 too young to decide this?"** Honestly, 18 is too young to decide a three-year degree is the *only* path too, and nobody questions that one. Ninety minutes a day, alongside college, is a low-risk way to test a skill without betting your entire future on it.

This is the assumption I want to break the hardest. Families in Sonipat often assume that anything digital or "modern" requires Delhi. It doesn't, and digital marketing is proof.

Our campus is at Model Town, Sonipat, with no daily commute required. ## A Realistic First-Year Timeline

Here's the honest sequence, month by month, for a Sonipat student starting right after 12th.

**Month 1:** Enrol alongside your college admission. Pick a class slot that doesn't clash with lectures.
**Months 1 to 4:** Complete the Full Stack program: Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, analytics, and AI tools, while earning your Google and Meta certifications.
**Months 4 to 5:** Build your portfolio with real campaign work, not dummy accounts.
**Months 5 to 6:** Start applying and pitching, aim for 30+ applications, plus local freelance outreach to small Sonipat businesses.
**Months 6 to 12:** Take on paid work, freelance or part-time, while your degree continues in the background.
