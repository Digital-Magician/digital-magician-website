Employers in Haryana keep asking for one person who can run campaigns and cut video. Not a filmmaker. Someone who can take a phone clip of a showroom, trim it, add captions and a hook, and have it live as an ad the same afternoon.

In our placements, that combination is worth about ₹4,000 to ₹7,000 a month extra at fresher level, and considerably more freelance. It takes a few weeks to learn to a usable standard, which is the best return on time in this field right now.

![A young marketer editing short video on a laptop with a phone on a tripod beside him](/blog-images/video-editing-and-digital-marketing-haryana.jpg)

## Why local employers want both in one person

Walk into a showroom, a gym or a clinic in Sonipat and ask who makes their reels. You will usually hear one of three answers: nobody, the owner's nephew, or a freelancer who charges per video and takes a week.

None of those work for advertising. Ad creative needs replacing constantly. A campaign that ran well for a fortnight fatigues, and the fix is new creative, not a new audience. If every new video needs a freelancer, a quotation and a week, the campaign dies while everyone waits.

So the employer wants the person running the ads to also be able to cut the video. It is not about artistry. It is about turnaround.

The freelance row is where it shows most. A client paying ₹8,000 a month for social media posts will pay ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 for posts plus video, because you have replaced two suppliers.

## How much editing you actually need

Far less than people assume. Here is the honest list for marketing work:

- **Trim and sequence.** Cut the dead air, order the clips. Most of the job.
- **Captions.** Burned in, readable on a phone, correct timing. Most social video is watched on mute.
- **A hook in the first second.** Not a title card. Movement, a face, or the thing the viewer came for.
- **Aspect ratios.** 9:16 for reels and stories, 1:1 or 4:5 for feed. Getting this wrong wastes the budget before anyone sees it.
- **Simple text and basic motion.** Enough to highlight an offer or a price.
- **Export settings that do not look terrible on a phone.** A small, specific skill that many editors get wrong.

That is two to three weeks of deliberate practice. Colour grading, sound design and cinematic transitions are a different craft and are not what advertising video is judged on.

## Start on the phone, not the laptop

This is the advice people argue with and then agree with six weeks later.

Most high performing ad video in India is shot and cut on a phone. CapCut on a phone will do everything in the list above, quickly, and it keeps you in the format the viewer will watch it in. Starting in Premiere Pro teaches you a workflow built for long form, which then has to be unlearned.

Move to desktop software when a client needs something longer or more layered. Not before.

## Where AI helps and where it does not

It helps with subtitles, rough cuts, removing backgrounds, cleaning audio and turning one long video into eight clips. That genuinely saves hours, and we teach it.

What it does not do is decide which three seconds matter. Watch two people cut the same footage and you will see the difference immediately: one picks the moment the customer smiles, the other picks the moment the logo appears. That judgement is the paid skill, and no tool supplies it. ## A realistic way in for someone in Sonipat

Pick one local business you already know. A friend's gym, a relative's shop, your own family business. Shoot thirty seconds on your phone. Cut it, caption it, post it.

Do that ten times and two things happen. You get fast, and you have a portfolio of real businesses rather than practice files. That portfolio is what gets you the first paid client, usually at ₹8,000 to ₹12,000 a month, often someone who saw the work you did for the gym.

Nobody will ask which software you used.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be a professional editor?**
No. Functional editing for ads takes two to three weeks of practice. Cinematic craft is a different job.

**How much more does the pairing pay?**
₹4,000 to ₹7,000 a month more at fresher level, and roughly double per client in freelance work.

**Which software first?**
Your phone, CapCut or similar. Desktop only when a client needs longer form.

**Can AI do the editing for me?**
It can do the labour. It cannot choose which seconds matter, and that is what you are paid for.

**Is a separate video editing course worth it?**
Only if you want to be an editor. If you want to be a marketer, learn it inside campaign work.
