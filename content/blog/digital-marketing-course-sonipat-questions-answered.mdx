## Before You Enrol

**What is a digital marketing course, actually?**
It's structured training in the skills that get a business found and bought from online: SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media, analytics, and increasingly AI tools layered on top of all of it. A good course isn't slides and theory, it's you running real campaigns under supervision until you can run them alone. I've written a full breakdown of the modules, typical duration, and what "digital marketing" actually covers in [what a digital marketing course is](/blog/what-is-a-digital-marketing-course).

**Who can join, and is there an eligibility requirement?**
Anyone after 12th, any stream, no coding background needed and no minimum percentage. I've trained BA graduates, BTech students, working professionals in their thirties, and housewives restarting after a decade-long gap, all in the same room. The only real requirement is that you show up consistently. The full breakdown of who qualifies and who this course actually suits is in [digital marketing course eligibility](/blog/digital-marketing-course-eligibility-qualifications).

**Is a ₹45,000 fee worth it, and how does it compare to other options in Sonipat?**
Our Full Stack program is ₹45,000, reduced from ₹60,000, which puts us at the top of the local fee range. That money buys founder-led teaching every single day, live client ad accounts with real budgets, small batches, and a written placement guarantee, not a WhatsApp group of job postings. Local budget centres charge ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 for generic slides and no live accounts. The full category-by-category comparison is in [digital marketing course fees in Sonipat](/blog/digital-marketing-course-fees-comparison-sonipat).

**Can I just learn for free instead of paying?**
You can start free, Google Digital Garage and Skillshop are genuinely useful and I recommend both. Where free learning breaks down is structure, accountability, and the live account experience. Most free learners get three weeks in, hit a wall on a real campaign decision, and stop. I've laid out exactly where free learning works and where it stalls in [free vs paid digital marketing course in Sonipat](/blog/free-vs-paid-digital-marketing-course-sonipat).

**How do I actually choose the right institute in Sonipat?**
Ask for placement proof with names and numbers, not a percentage on a poster. Ask who teaches your batch and whether that person runs live campaigns for real clients. Ask about batch size, because a room of 60 watching a projector is a lecture, not training. I've built a full checklist and the exact questions to ask any institute you're considering in [how to choose the best digital marketing institute in Sonipat](/blog/how-to-choose-best-digital-marketing-institute-sonipat).

**Should I do it online or offline?**
Offline gets you into the room, correction happens faster, and accountability is higher when you've physically shown up. Online works well if you're disciplined and travel is a genuine obstacle, not just a preference. We run the same live batch online at the same fee, so the content doesn't change, only the format does. The honest tradeoffs are in [online vs offline digital marketing course in Sonipat](/blog/online-vs-offline-digital-marketing-course-sonipat).
