There are digital marketing jobs in the Kundli and Rai belt, and almost none of them are advertised. The hiring comes from manufacturers and exporters who sell through trade enquiries, from logistics firms along the highway, and from local clinics, schools and showrooms. Freshers here earn ₹15,000 to ₹22,000 a month, and the roles come with more responsibility than an equivalent job in a large NCR agency.

I have placed students into both. The honest version of this comparison is not the one you get from a brochure.

![The industrial belt near Sonipat, with warehouses, a service road and a young professional walking with a laptop bag](/blog-images/digital-marketing-jobs-kundli-rai-sonipat.jpg)

## Who is actually hiring along this stretch

The belt from Kundli through Rai to Bahalgarh is industrial first. That shapes the kind of marketing work available.

**Manufacturers and exporters.** Plastics, auto components, textiles, food processing. Most of them live on IndiaMART, trade portals and a website nobody has touched since 2019. They need someone who can keep listings current, respond to enquiries fast, and run small campaigns that produce bulk orders. Unglamorous, and steady.

**Logistics and warehousing.** The highway brings transport and storage companies. Their marketing need is mostly B2B lead generation and a presence that looks credible to a buyer in another state.

**Local service businesses.** Clinics, diagnostic labs, coaching centres, gyms, boutiques, restaurants in Model Town and around the Sector 14 and 15 markets. This is the most common first client for a freelancer, and it is where Meta ads and local SEO earn their keep.

**Education.** The Rajiv Gandhi Education City campuses and private schools nearby. Admissions marketing is seasonal and intense, which suits people who like deadlines.

## What those jobs pay in 2026

| Role | Kundli and Rai belt | Delhi NCR |
|---|---|---|
| Fresher, in-house marketing | ₹15,000 to ₹22,000 | ₹18,000 to ₹30,000 |
| 2 years, running campaigns | ₹28,000 to ₹40,000 | ₹45,000 to ₹65,000 |
| Managing a small team | ₹45,000 to ₹70,000 | ₹70,000 to ₹1,20,000 |
| Freelance, per local client | ₹8,000 to ₹20,000 a month | ₹15,000 to ₹40,000 a month |
| Daily commute | 15 to 40 minutes | 3 to 4 hours, or relocate |

The local numbers are lower. They are also paid to someone who sleeps at home, spends nothing on rent and keeps roughly fifteen hours a week that a Gurgaon commuter does not have. Two local freelance clients on top of a local job closes most of the gap, and that is a realistic thing to do in your first year.

## The part nobody tells freshers

In a large NCR agency, a fresher usually gets one narrow slice of the work. You write ad copy, or you pull reports, or you schedule posts. It is good training in a narrow discipline and slow training in everything else.

At a manufacturer in Rai, you are often the entire marketing department. You will be asked to fix the website, run the ads, handle the IndiaMART enquiries and explain to the owner why the enquiries are not converting. It is messy and you will be out of your depth regularly.

That mess is the fastest way to become employable. Eighteen months of doing everything badly and then better gives you a portfolio that a Gurgaon agency will hire at a level above fresher. I have watched this happen often enough to stop treating it as the consolation prize.

## How to find work that is never posted

Job portals will not help you here, because these owners do not use them for a marketing hire. What works is direct and slightly uncomfortable.

Pick ten companies within fifteen kilometres. Look at their website, their Google listing and their IndiaMART page. Write down two specific things that are wrong: no reviews on the Google profile, a website that takes nine seconds to load, product photos from a phone in bad light, enquiries that go to an inbox nobody checks.

Then write to the owner, not to a generic email. Two sentences on what you noticed, one sentence offering to fix the first one, and nothing else. Most will ignore you. Two will reply. That ratio is enough.

This works because you are not asking for a job, you are pointing at money being left on the table. The [local SEO guide for Haryana businesses](/blog/local-seo-haryana-small-business-guide-2026) is a reasonable checklist to audit them against before you write.
